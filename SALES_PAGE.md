# POD Launch Audit Kit

Stop guessing which products are ready to sell.

The POD Launch Audit Kit checks your merch catalog before you waste hours uploading broken listings. It gives you a simple readiness report showing what is ready, what is blocked, and what to fix next.

## What you get

- POD Profit Scout readiness checker
- Product metadata template
- Launch checklist
- Sample catalog format
- Report output you can hand to an assistant, publisher, or agent

## Best for

- Etsy sellers
- Printify and Printful sellers
- Sticker shops
- AI-art product builders
- Small teams launching merch drops
